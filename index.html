Who We Are

Happisoy Global is a global B2B supply platform specializing in wholesale and outsourcing services across lighting, consumer electronics, industrial hardware components, and authorized aviation parts.

We serve clients worldwide — including lighting distributors, retailers, home improvement chains, digital device dealers, e-commerce sellers, supermarkets, and public-private partnership (PPP) projects.

Our goal: To become the go-to B2B platform for commercial, industrial, and individual buyers, offering one-stop procurement and customized wholesale solutions.
